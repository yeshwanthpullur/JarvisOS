# Product Requirements Summary

JARVIS OS must remain local-first, permission-aware, provider-neutral, bounded, test-driven, and honest about unavailable capabilities. It must not silently record, mutate memory, execute privileged actions, expose secrets, or turn provider availability into authorization.

The complete product history is preserved in the repository roadmap and prompt history. Missing Engineering Step definitions are not inferred here.

## Recovered Direction

The project aims toward useful, legitimate automation for research, documents, content preparation, coding, engineering workflows, data processing, and reusable workflows. Cost-aware routing prefers local, then free, then low-cost, then paid options. No subscription, credential, or external service is mandatory for the current local foundation.
