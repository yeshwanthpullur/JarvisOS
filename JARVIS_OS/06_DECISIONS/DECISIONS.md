# Decisions

## 2026-09-08: Engineering numbering is separate

Prompt/Phase numbering and Engineering Step numbering are distinct. The canonical engineering ledger must not invent or infer missing steps.

## 2026-09-08: Missing definitions remain explicit

Steps without recoverable authoritative definitions are recorded as `UNKNOWN / NOT RECOVERED` and are not implemented.

## 2026-09-08: Authority boundaries remain unchanged

Workers, providers, external content, MCP discovery, and tool availability do not gain execution authority. Existing Policy, Approval, Broker, memory, privacy, and local-first boundaries remain authoritative.
