# Health Monitoring

Registered, ready, healthy, available, and operational are distinct states. Component health includes a bounded score, availability, transitions, dependencies, resources, provider references, warnings, and safe errors. Aggregate health degrades when a required component is degraded/offline and fails when a component reports failure.

Reports are validated and treated as untrusted metadata. They exclude credentials, private payloads, conversation history, documents, webpages, and raw provider responses.
