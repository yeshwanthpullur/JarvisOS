# Research Evidence

Every retrieved source has a provider, normalized reference, source type, retrieval state, freshness, quality signals, and trust class. Evidence chunks retain source IDs and bounded location metadata. Claims link to supporting evidence and may be `unsupported`, `single_source`, `corroborated`, `strong_primary_support`, `conflicting`, `stale`, or `unknown`.

Citations are emitted only from actual normalized source records. Missing author, publisher, publication date, or version stays unknown. Independent hosts can support corroboration; copied or syndicated material must not be treated as independent confirmation. Contradictions and version/date differences remain visible instead of being silently resolved.
