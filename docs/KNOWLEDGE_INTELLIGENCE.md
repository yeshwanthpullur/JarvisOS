# Knowledge Intelligence

Knowledge Intelligence is a controlled evidence retrieval subsystem, distinct from Persistent Memory, conversation context, and authoritative task/goal state. Sources are registered explicitly and remain untrusted evidence even after local indexing. The initial backend is lightweight and in-memory; lexical retrieval is ready, while semantic and hybrid retrieval remain unavailable until a governed embedding provider is configured.

Source registration, ingestion, reindex, and removal are controlled operations. There is no automatic research admission, crawler, home-directory indexing, all-repository indexing, scheduled ingestion, or direct knowledge-to-memory path.
