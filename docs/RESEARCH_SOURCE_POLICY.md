# Research Source Policy

Prefer official documentation, standards, government sources, papers, official repositories, and primary announcements for current technical claims. Secondary and community sources can add practical context but are labelled accordingly.

Research does not bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, bot controls, robots/access policy, or rate limits. It does not crawl sites, enumerate accounts, harvest profiles, follow uncontrolled pagination, or download large datasets. URL credentials and credential-shaped query parameters are rejected. Unsupported or inaccessible content is reported as such.
