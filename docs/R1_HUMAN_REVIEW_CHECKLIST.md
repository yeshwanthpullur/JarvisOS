# R1 Human Review Checklist

Reviewer role: QA lead or designated human delivery reviewer. Do not record a personal name unless required by an authorized process.

- requirements coverage
- factual correctness
- source quality
- citation/provenance
- formatting
- completeness
- privacy
- obvious hallucinations
- unsupported claims

Allowed outcomes: `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Even a passing automated gate remains `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. `APPROVED_FOR_SYNTHETIC_PORTFOLIO_ONLY` is not client delivery approval.
